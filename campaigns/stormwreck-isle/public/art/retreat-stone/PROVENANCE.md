# Basalto costero v1

Textura nueva generada con la herramienta integrada ImageGen el 21/10/2026, como material de VTT_AMBIENCE para el Retiro del Dragón. No es fotografía ni referencia canónica de la aventura. Se aplica a geometría 3D y no sustituye el mapa.

Fuente conservada: `basalt-coastal-albedo-source-v1.png`. Ejecución: `basalt-coastal-albedo-v1.webp`, 512 × 512, RGB. Sólo se ha redimensionado y comprimido la salida elegida. Normales y rugosidad se reutilizan de la biblioteca existente; no son mapas escaneados.

## Prompt exacto

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square seamless PBR BASE COLOR TEXTURE for volcanic basalt used on an existing modular 3D D&D coastal cliff in Babylon.js. Generate a production material tile, NOT a scene, concept illustration or render of an object. Orthographic straight-on view of a continuous, fairly flat natural basalt surface filling the entire square edge to edge. Matte medium cool slate gray and charcoal, restrained warm gray mineral weathering, low to medium contrast. Quiet broad mineral patches, sparse narrow irregular cooling fractures with a subtle vertical tendency, fine mineral grain that reads close up without noisy speckling at distance. Fractures irregular and staggered, not a regular masonry or hexagon grid. Surface represents about 3.3 metres of stone. Flat, even, neutral illumination: no directional shadows, no highlight, no ambient occlusion baked into color, no vignette. Tileable seamlessly in both directions; opposite edges must match. A calm handcrafted game material with believable mineral structure, not cartoon outlines. No floor tiles, bricks, isolated boulders, big hexagonal blocks, lava, moss, grass, water, architecture, objects, text or watermark. Opaque square image.
