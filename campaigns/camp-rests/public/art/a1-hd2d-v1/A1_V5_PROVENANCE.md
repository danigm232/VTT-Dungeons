# A1 V5 · procedencia del atlas

`a1-material-atlas-v5.png` es un atlas de 2048 × 2048 generado el 27/09/2026 con ImageGen integrado. Contiene cuatro superficies para la reconstrucción canónica irregular de A1: losas de la plaza, pared interior tallada, estratos del acantilado y suelo de las celdas. Se usa directamente como atlas para evitar cuatro descargas y conservar memoria.

Original conservado por Codex: `exec-be98c219-18c2-4360-a68a-3d4d1df7d7cf.png`.

## Prompt

> Create a single square 2048x2048 production texture atlas for a stylized HD-2D fantasy VTT diorama, inspired by hand-painted JRPG environments. Orthographic top-down material swatches only, no objects, no characters, no text, no frames, no lighting direction, no perspective. Exact 2x2 grid of four equally sized edge-to-edge seamless-looking swatches: TOP LEFT weathered pale limestone plaza floor with worn flagstones, packed earth in joints, subtle chips and warm beige/gray variation; TOP RIGHT carved sea-cliff interior wall with layered pale limestone, tool marks, natural cracks, darker crevices and moss traces; BOTTOM LEFT vertical coastal cliff strata with warm gray limestone, erosion grooves, salt stains and small dark fissures; BOTTOM RIGHT humble monastic cell floor made of irregular stone slabs, dust, wear paths and restrained brown-gray variation. Painterly but highly detailed, readable at distance, realistic material depth, consistent color palette, no harsh baked shadows, no glossy highlights. The four quadrants must have clearly different surface language while remaining stylistically coherent.

Las texturas son VTT_AMBIENCE: no alteran reglas, obstáculos ni la topología canónica.
